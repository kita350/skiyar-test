Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy describes how SKIYAR — Individual Entrepreneur Skino Nikita Vladimirovich (Tax ID 20453381, Kotayk, Armenia; "we") handles information in connection with our services and our internal software application ("the App").

1. What the App does

The App analyses the public online presence of small businesses — restaurants, guest houses, hotels and beauty salons — to prepare consultations on automation, online booking and websites. It is used only internally by us.

2. What information we process

  • Publicly available business information from public web sources, including maps, business directories, business websites and public business pages on social networks: business name, category, address, business phone, website, links to business social media profiles, opening hours, public ratings and review counts, follower counts and the date of the latest public post.
  • Derived observations, for example whether a business website opens or whether online booking is offered.
  • Data received through the Instagram Graph API (Business Discovery) about public Instagram business and creator accounts: username, profile description, website link, follower and media counts, and dates of recent posts. We do not store post texts or captions; we store only our conclusions and the date of the check.

We do not collect information about private individuals, do not access private accounts or messages, and do not process special categories of personal data.

3. Why we process it

Solely to identify businesses that may benefit from our services and to prepare a relevant, personalised proposal. Every message is written and sent manually by us; the App does not send messages automatically.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or transfer this information to third parties. Data obtained from Meta platforms is used only as described above and in accordance with the Meta Platform Terms.

5. Storage and retention

Data is stored on our own computer with restricted access and backups. Records of businesses we have not been in contact with are deleted after 12 months.

6. Your rights and data deletion

If you represent a business and want us to delete its data or never contact you, email skiyar.technologies@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete the data and add the business to our do-not-contact list within 30 days and confirm by email.
