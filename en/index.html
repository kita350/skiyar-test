            What owners ask us

            I have Instagram. Why would I need a site?

            The site isn’t the point. The point is that a client books by themselves at 23:00 instead of waiting for a reply until morning. Instagram stays; the booking button goes right into your profile.

            It’s expensive.

            Try the calculator above: usually a couple of recovered visits a month cover the fee.

            I already use DIKIDI or Altegio.

            Great, we work with them: we connect them to your site and Instagram and set up reminders in Armenian. You keep the software.

            Why a subscription?

            Because we’re responsible for the result every month, rather than handing over a site and disappearing. If you don’t like subscriptions, there’s a one-time site for 160 000–200 000 ֏ with 3 months of support included, then 20 000 ֏ a month or 204 000 ֏ a year.

            What if I want to leave?

            The contract is for 12 months, but you can leave any time with 30 days’ notice: no penalty and no payment for the remaining months. If you want to keep the site before 12 months, you buy it out: 160 000 ֏ − (months paid × 10 000 ֏), e.g. 100 000 ֏ after 6 months. After 12 months the site is yours for free.

            What do “edits up to 2 hours a month” cover?

            Updating prices, services, photos and texts. If you need more, we’ll quote the price before we start.

            Is the AI front desk working already?

            Not yet. We’re testing it and we don’t sell it as a finished product. For now, at night the client gets an auto-reply with the booking link.

            Who are you?

            SKIYAR is a sole proprietorship in Armenia, a small team. Our sample sites are on this page; the contract and invoices are official, in the proprietorship’s name.
